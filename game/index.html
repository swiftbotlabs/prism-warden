Prism Warden: move the Warden lens with WASD or the arrow keys, hold the left mouse button to cast a beam toward the cursor, and pick its colour with 1, 2, 3 or the mouse wheel (Ember red triangle, Verdant green square, Azure blue circle). Only light of a Shade's own colour harms it; mirrors bounce the beam. Survive ten waves and the boss THE ECLIPSE, choosing one of three upgrades after each wave. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, R restarts.
